Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Basant Bazaar ("Basant Bazaar", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we access, collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use the Basant Bazaar mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App").

Basant Bazaar is a marketplace application intended to help users browse, list, buy, sell, and manage Basant-related products and accessories. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Important: This Privacy Policy should match the actual functionality and third-party SDKs included in the version of the App published on Google Play. We may update this policy when our App features or data practices change.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and profile information

When you create or use a Basant Bazaar account, we may collect:

  • Mobile phone number for account registration and OTP verification
  • Name and user profile details that you provide
  • User role, such as Buyer, Seller, or Manufacturer
  • Profile image, if you choose to upload one
  • Communication preferences and account settings

1.2 Seller and manufacturer information

If you register as a Seller or Manufacturer, we may collect additional information needed to operate marketplace features, including:

  • Shop, business, or manufacturer name
  • Business address, city, and pickup or delivery details
  • CNIC information, where requested for identity or account verification
  • Licence or certificate information, if you choose to provide it
  • Product categories, listings, inventory details, and related business information

1.3 Orders and delivery information

When you browse, place, receive, sell, or manage an order, we may collect or generate:

  • Product and order details
  • Delivery address and recipient information
  • Order status, transaction reference details, and fulfilment information
  • Messages or support requests you send through available App features

1.4 Photos and uploaded content

The App may allow users to upload product photos, profile images, verification documents, or other content. We collect the files you choose to upload in order to provide the requested marketplace, profile, listing, and verification functions.

1.5 Device, diagnostics, and usage information

We may collect limited technical and usage information, including:

  • Device identifiers, app instance identifiers, or similar identifiers
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, and settings
  • Crash logs, performance diagnostics, and security-related events
  • App interactions, screens viewed, features used, and session information
  • IP-address-derived approximate location or network information, where generated by service providers for security, analytics, or service operation

1.6 Camera and QR/barcode scanning

If you choose to use QR code or barcode scanning features, the App may request access to your device camera. Camera access is used only to scan codes when you start that feature. We do not use camera access for unrelated purposes.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the App to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts through phone-number OTP verification
  • Provide marketplace functions, including listings, browsing, ordering, delivery, and profile features
  • Enable Seller and Manufacturer onboarding and verification-related features
  • Display product listings, shop information, and content that users choose to make available through the marketplace
  • Process and manage orders, customer support, disputes, and service requests
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, spam, security incidents, and unauthorised activity
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, improve, and secure the App
  • Analyse App performance and usage trends
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share or make information available only as necessary to provide App functions, operate our services, comply with the law, or protect users and the App.

3.1 Other marketplace users

Certain information is visible to other users when it is needed for marketplace functionality. For example, a Seller's shop name, product listings, product images, city, and business details may be visible to Buyers. Delivery or order information may be shared with the relevant Buyer, Seller, or Manufacturer to fulfil an order.

We do not intentionally display sensitive identity information, such as CNIC details or private verification documents, publicly to other users.

3.2 Service providers

We use trusted service providers to help operate the App. Depending on the services enabled in the released App version, these providers may process information on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or related Firebase services, for account authentication, database storage, file storage, notifications, analytics, diagnostics, and App operation.
  • Google Play services, where applicable, for distribution, security, performance, and related platform services.
  • Payment, delivery, communication, or verification providers only if and when those services are enabled and required to fulfil the requested feature or transaction.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy terms and policies. Firebase information is available at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.3 Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if we reasonably believe it is necessary to comply with applicable law, a valid legal request, prevent fraud or abuse, protect the security of the App, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Basant Bazaar, our users, or others.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect user information. Data transmitted between the App and supported services is intended to use encrypted connections, such as HTTPS/TLS.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device, account access, and OTP verification code secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the App, operate marketplace services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain required business or security records.

For example, active account information may be retained while your account remains active. Certain order, transaction, dispute, fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records may be retained after account deletion where necessary and permitted by applicable law.

Uploaded product content may be removed when the related listing or account is deleted, subject to technical backup cycles and any retention required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes.

6. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may update certain profile details through the App. You can also request deletion of your Basant Bazaar account and associated personal data.

7. Children’s Privacy

Basant Bazaar is not designed for children. The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. International Processing

Our service providers, including cloud service providers, may process or store information in locations outside your city, province, or country. Where information is transferred, we take reasonable steps designed to protect it in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, App functionality, legal requirements, or security measures. The updated version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
