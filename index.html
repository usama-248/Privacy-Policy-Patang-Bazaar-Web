Account and data deletion request

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Basant Bazaar account and associated personal data by completing the form below.

Important: After your account is deleted, you may lose access to your profile, saved addresses, listings, order history, and other account-related features.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter the mobile number registered with your Basant Bazaar account.
  2. Enter your full name and email address, if available.
  3. Submit the account deletion request.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership.
  5. Requests are normally processed within 30 days after verification.

Data deleted

  • Basant Bazaar account and profile information
  • Registered phone number, name, and contact information
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Seller or manufacturer profile details, where applicable
  • Product listings and uploaded product images, where applicable
  • CNIC and licence details, where applicable

Data that may be retained

Limited order, transaction, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legal-compliance records may be retained only where necessary.

Such records are securely stored and are not used for marketing. Account deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days after account ownership verification.
